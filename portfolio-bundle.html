About Me

A passionate student building smart systems and leading innovation

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Industrial Technology major in Computer Technology at Batangas State University. As a Cum Laude graduate (Batch 2025-2026) and Dean's Lister (2023-2024), I combine academic excellence with hands-on technical skills in Arduino-based system development.

My passion lies in creating IoT solutions and automated systems that solve real-world problems. From smart bins that open automatically to rice vending machines and smoke alarm systems with CCTV monitoring—I love turning ideas into working prototypes.

Beyond technical work, I serve as Class President, Organization Officer, and CPET Director of TECHLINK, where I lead teams, organize events, and promote collaboration among students. I believe in combining technical expertise with strong leadership and communication skills.
